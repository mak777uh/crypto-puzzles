Frequently asked questions

Answers to most common questions.

Do I need a crypto wallet?

You do not need a crypto wallet to use this platform! However, if you wish to pay in Cardano or transfer the NFT into your custody, you will need a wallet for that.

How do I create a Cardano wallet?

To create a wallet, you can download Daedelus or use the Yoroi light wallet as a browser extension or phone app.

Can I try it for free?

It is free to create an account, but you must purchase minting credits to create tokens.

Do you have hidden fees?

There are NO hidden fees. Credit/Debit transactions cost a bit higher than a simple crypto transaction due to the additional logistics of paying in credit/fiat to help cover the processing fees on our end. Although you should be aware that there is a small transaction fee to move tokens off this platform.

What is the transaction fee?

The transaction fee costs 1 minting credit and does not take into account the amount of tokens you'd like to move. Once initiated, you have 24 hours to bundle in as many additional tokens before the transaction is sent off to be processed.

What are the payment methods you accept?

We accept both credit and crypto, although additional surchages apply when paying in credit.

What is your refund policy?

Unfortunately, once the token is minted it is impossible to issue a refund.

How can I recover my account?

Unfortunately, our account system uses unrecoverable cryptographic identities that are rooted in randomly generated mnuemonics. There is no way to retrieve the originating mnuemonic from our end.

What are the tokens that I can mint?

Currently, standard users can mint documents and virtual art pieces, but expect music tokens and much more soon!
