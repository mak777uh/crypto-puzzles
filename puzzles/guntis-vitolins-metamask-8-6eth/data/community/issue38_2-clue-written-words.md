Read plainly, this says the audio is not a source: a viewer asks whether he
needs a word he could not make out in the audio, and the author says only
written words are needed. If `clues/author-posts.md` does not already carry
this reply, it is worth adding, because it would retire the auto-caption half
of lead 1 before anyone sweeps it.

Scope, stated conservatively. The reply was given about the *spoken audio*. It
does not obviously settle whether text rendered on screen counts as "written",
and cjmcdaniel's frame read (2026-09-04) is unaffected either way. It also
predates hints 4 and 5, which are themselves spoken — but those are hints that
*name* words, not words to be transcribed, so there is no contradiction.
