HOW TO PLAY

Each hunt is unique, but gameplay from first clue to the treasure stays simple.

01
Area

See the search area

Every hunt begins with a clearly defined search area. In many hunts, the search area shrinks daily.

Free players can follow the shrinking search boundary and know exactly where the hunt is still live.

AREA UPDATED
02
Proof

Watch the treasure

Trailcam imagery confirms the treasure is still waiting to be found.

Fresh, timestamped images turn every update into evidence--and sometimes a clue of its own.

HUNT ACTIVE
03
Clues

Solve the clues

Follow maps, riddles, history and location clues. Unlock premium tools and deeper insights with Veil Edge.

Explore the evidence, compare potential solves and use HuntMapper to organize your search plan.

PLAN YOUR SOLVE
04
Guess

Search outside or guess online

Veil Pro and Veil Edge players can drop a pin to guess one location each day.

Once the treasure is found, your guesses process into points for a public leaderboard. In some hunts, a qualifying online guess wins a portion of the reward.

DAILY GUESS LOCKED
05
Find

Find it first

Be the first player to touch the treasure and the reward is yours.

The hunt ends the moment the treasure is found.

Key rules

The short version of what keeps every hunt fair, safe, and fun.

  • Participants under 18 need a parent or legal guardian’s consent and direct supervision.
  • Veil Free, Veil Pro and Veil Edge players are eligible to win.
  • Account registration is required before participating.
  • The first eligible person to touch the treasure wins.
  • The treasure must always be acquired peacefully and responsibly.
  • Players must research and follow all rules and restrictions for every park or public land they enter.
  • Rewards must be claimed within 30 days of the find.
