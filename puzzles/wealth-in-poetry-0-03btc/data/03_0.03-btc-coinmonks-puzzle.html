0.03 BTC Wealth in Poetry Puzzle

Status: UNSOLVED
Prize: 0.03 BTC
Owner: Trithemius
Start Date: 2019-02-11
Address: 1K4ezpLybootYF23TM4a8Y4NyP7auysnRo

Description

In February 2019, an author using the name Trithemius published Securing Wealth in Poetry in the Coinmonks publication. The article explains how a cryptocurrency recovery phrase can be hidden inside an ordinary story, letter, or poem.

The author states that every word required to access a wallet containing 0.03 BTC appears somewhere in the article. The wallet was later topped up and now contains 0.0312463 BTC at the address 1K4ezpLybootYF23TM4a8Y4NyP7auysnRo.

How it works

The article calls the technique a trithemian seed. A secret numeric key selects words at specific positions in an innocent-looking text. Reading those words in the selected order reconstructs the wallet's recovery phrase.

Two worked examples explain the general technique, but the numeric key used for the funded wallet is not disclosed. Searching only for consecutive BIP39 words has not produced the correct phrase.

Solution

No verified solution has been published. The prize address has received 3,124,630 satoshis in two transactions and has never spent them.
