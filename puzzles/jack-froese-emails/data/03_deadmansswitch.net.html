Dead Man's Switch

Why?

Bad things happen. Sometimes, they happen to you. If something does happen, you might wish there was something you had told the people around you. How you feel, what you regret, what you wish you had said. Or maybe you just want to make sure your pets are taken care of promptly.
For that, you need a dead man's switch.

Build my switch

How?

This is how this works. You write a few e-mails and choose the recipients. These emails are stored privately until they're sent. Your switch will email you every so often, asking you to show that you are fine by clicking a link. If something were to... happen... to you, your switch would then send the emails you wrote to the recipients you specified. Sort of an "electronic will", one could say.

When?

The notifications are sent at certain intervals (by email, the Android app, Telegram, or browser push notification). You can set the intervals to whatever you want, from one day to years. By default, the switch will notify you 30, 45, and 52 days after you last showed signs of life (free accounts are limited to 1 day after). If you don't respond to any of those notifications, all your messages will be sent 60 days after your last checkin.

How much?

A free account sends its message only to your own address, as a test, so you can see exactly what your recipients would get. To send your messages to the people they are meant for, upgrade to premium: up to 100 recipients across 100 messages. Yearly plans also get file attachments.

What if?

Dead Man's Switch has been running continuously since 2008 and is actively maintained. We notify you over multiple channels (email, the Android app, Telegram, and browser push), so you never miss a check-in. If the service ever shuts down, you'll receive an email well in advance, so you can migrate elsewhere.
