Mapnificent Treasure Hunt [MTH202610]

  • mth_admin
  • September 28, 2026
  • 0 Comment

We start our second game of Mapnificent Treasure Hunt [MTH202610] earlier because we have a software upgrade next week. We do not want it to interrupt the Manificent Treasure Hunt game.  The goal of the game is to find answers to five riddles related to the same story.  They are all labeled with the token [MTH202610]. When you find all five answers, you can submit them to the email address [email protected].  The first contestant to submit the five correct answers wins prize money of $400 USD ($200 from previous game without a winner).  The contest will close at the end of the second month.  The winner will be announced in the third month.  You can find the rules of the contest and the tips for solving the riddles in the links below.  Here is the first riddle of the game.

我们即将开启 Mapnificent 寻宝游戏（Mapnificent Treasure Hunt）的第二轮活动 [MTH202610]。本次活动的目标是解答五个与同一故事相关的谜题，所有谜题均标有 [MTH202610] 这一标识。集齐全部五个答案后，请将其发送至邮箱 [email protected]。首位提交全部五个正确答案的参赛者将赢得 400 美元的奖金（其中包括上一轮因无人获胜而累积的 200 美元奖金）。本次活动将于第二个月结束时截止，获胜者名单将在第三个月公布。您可以通过下方链接查看比赛规则及解谜提示。以下是本轮游戏的第一个谜题。

First riddle:
大漠孤烟映佛光，千年洞窟蕴珍藏。
飞天漫舞云裳动，丝路文明万古扬。

Translated by Google:
A solitary plume of smoke rises against the desert backdrop, illuminated by a divine Buddha-light; ancient grottoes harbor treasures spanning a millennium.
Celestial maidens dance in flowing, cloud-like robes; as the civilization of the Silk Road shines on through the ages.
