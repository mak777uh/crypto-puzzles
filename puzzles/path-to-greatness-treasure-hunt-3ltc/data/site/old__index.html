Path to Greatness: Treasure Hunt

What the heck is this thing?

It's kinda two things:

It's a gameplay demo for Path to Greatness

It's free, and you can download it right now. Even better, if you're on a Windows PC, you can actually play it after downloading it. In this demo you can parkour your way around an obstacle course while collecting orbs. Collect all the orbs and a timer will stop. See if you can beat my time of 8:10 with lava turned on. Oh yeah, you can turn on lava. This is an early gameplay demo, but I think it's fun, and I wanted to share with you what I've got so far. Plus I'm using it as a delivery system for something else...

It's an online treasure hunt

And you can win Litecoin by solving the puzzle. Clues can be found inside the demo, and will lead you to a Litecoin wallet's private key. Once found, take your prize by sending the Litecoin to your own Litecoin wallet. Good luck, and have fun!
